Before You Compare

Questions Worth Asking Before Renewal

Use your own policy documents and written quotes. These prompts are a starting point for a conversation with a licensed professional.

What Does My Deductible Mean?

Find your hurricane deductible on the declarations page. Ask how it applies to a covered hurricane loss. Florida DFS hurricane deductible guide.

Have I Considered Flood Coverage?

Most homeowners policies exclude flood damage. Review a separate flood policy and its exclusions. FEMA flood insurance guide.

What Roof Documents Are Needed?

Ask your insurer for its current inspection and roof documentation requirements in writing. Do not infer eligibility from a roof-age headline.

Am I Comparing Equivalent Quotes?

Put coverage limits, exclusions, deductibles, and total premiums side by side. Florida consumer insurance shopping guide.

What Does My Renewal Notice Say?

Keep the notice and ask your existing agent to explain any changed terms, deadlines, or next steps. Avoid assuming that an online estimate applies to your property.

Who Can Help With a Current Policy?

Contact your current insurer or a licensed insurance professional. Palm Beach Coverage is not currently providing agency services.

Coverage Topics

Prepare for a More Useful Insurance Conversation

These are topics to discuss, not coverage offers or recommendations for a particular policy.

Homeowners Insurance

Bring your declarations page and ask what property, liability, limits, and exclusions your policy includes.

Flood Insurance

NFIP building and belongings coverage have separate terms and deductibles. Ask which parts you need to consider. FEMA flood insurance guide.

Windstorm and Hurricane

A hurricane deductible is not a general rule for every named storm. Ask your insurer to explain the policy trigger and applicable deductible. Florida DFS hurricane deductible guide.

Auto Insurance

Describe your drivers and vehicle use to a licensed professional. Ask for an explanation of each proposed coverage and exclusion.

Commercial Property

Bring information about your building, business use, equipment, and lease obligations. Ask what a proposed policy includes and leaves out.

Umbrella and Excess Liability

Ask how proposed liability limits relate to your existing policies, and request written confirmation of any underlying coverage requirements.

Read the Source, Then Check Your Policy

Read Your Policy Details

Ask about replacement cost, actual cash value, and exclusions before comparing premiums. Florida consumer insurance shopping guide.

Review Flood Policy Terms

Check building and contents protection, exclusions, and when a proposed flood policy would start. FEMA flood insurance guide.

Understand Hurricane Deductibles

Florida DFS explains calendar-year hurricane deductibles and notes that surplus-lines policies may differ. Florida DFS hurricane deductible guide.

Ask About Discounts

Discounts depend on insurer requirements. Ask which documented improvements or multiple-policy options apply; no savings percentage is promised. Florida consumer insurance shopping guide.
